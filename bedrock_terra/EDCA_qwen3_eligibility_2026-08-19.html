Correction, and a small defect in the release

An earlier version of this page reported zero schema errors, taking the figure from the runner’s own record. Re-deriving with the frozen scorer finds one rejection in 360: CALL51-0000192 returned a reason field of 665 characters against the schema maximum of 600, so schema_errors() returns INVALID_REASON. Under intention-to-evaluate it counts as incorrect, which is the correct handling.

The two disagree because the runner’s terminal status does not incorporate the scorer’s schema check: the call is recorded COMPLETED_VALID with an empty errors list for an object the scorer rejects. This is the same family as defect 001 — a divergence between what the runner records and what the scorer decides — and is benign at this rate, but it is a real inconsistency in the release and is reported rather than smoothed over. The invalid-output rate is 1/360 = 0.0028, against a warning line of 0.05: eligibility is unaffected.

4. Reasoning effort: the selection is forced

Decision 3 excludes any setting that truncates input, exceeds the frozen truncation limit, or falls below the frozen validity limit, then takes the lowest surviving effort. This endpoint exposes low and medium; high exceeded the 180-second probe timeout.

low clears steps 1–3 with every monitor at zero. Because it is the lowest setting available, no higher setting could outrank it, so none was run and none needed to be. The frozen value is low, chosen by the algorithm rather than by an operator, which removes the tuning hazard the decision was written to prevent.

5. The retry policy, proven in production

Three calls hit a transient DNS failure. All three recovered, one of them only on its third attempt — the policy exercising its full depth rather than its happy path.

What this does and does not establish

It establishes that the endpoint can carry the unchanged benchmark: the payload fits, all three conditions transmit intact, the frozen parser and scorer work without modification, transport faults recover, and the full reserved matrix is affordable and quick. Under Decision 1 that is the whole of the eligibility question.

It establishes nothing about whether the effect replicates. The development contrasts were opened only after the record was signed, and in any case the confirmatory claim ruled by Decision 4 is the reserved split, which remains untouched. The value of the sequence is that this arm was retained on operational grounds before any result could influence the choice.
