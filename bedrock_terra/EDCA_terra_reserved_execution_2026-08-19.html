Declarations for the annex

Three facts about this run condition how it may be reported. They are recorded in the authorisation file and repeated here so they travel with the data.

  1. The decision to execute followed the Qwen3 result

    Terra was pre-registered as the saturation control in the 19 August amendment, before any reserved outcome existed, so this is not arm selection by measured headroom. But the decision to spend on the run was taken after the Qwen3 reserved outcome had been inspected and found positive, and that conditioning belongs in the record.

  2. The ceiling does not go away in the reserved split

    Terra's development baseline B0 was 98.33%, leaving about 1.7 pp of headroom. The primary contrast B1−B0 cannot differ from zero on this arm by construction. This run must not be reported as a replication of the primary claim; its pre-specified value is the cross-model M0−B0 signal and confirmation of the saturation classification on reserved rather than development data alone.

  3. The effort selector was not blinded on this arm

    Terra's condition-level development results had already been inspected when reasoning_effort was recorded, so the setting was recorded rather than blindly selected. The selection is degenerate in any case — low is the lowest exposed setting and clears every gate at floor — and no dated version pin exists for this model, so the run window is the only bound on the served version.

What is needed next

Inspection requires a signed record naming reserved_terra, on the pattern used for Qwen3. Before requesting it, note that the ceiling bounds what the run can return: a null primary contrast here is a property of the instrument on this model, established in advance, and not evidence about the DCA.

The confirmatory claim of the amendment rests on Qwen3, and is unaffected by whatever this arm shows.
