This is description, and it must not become a claim

You ruled on 19 August that arms may not be chosen by measured headroom, and that the same objection applies at subgroup level — conditioning inclusion on a statistic related to the outcome. That ruling kills the TEMPORAL_GAP subgroup contrast as an endpoint, including as an exploratory one, and it kills it here too: the family was identified after reading the outcomes. It is reported to explain why the arm saturates, which is what a control is for. Nothing in this section is a test.

The honest reading of the small B1 penalty on the other four families — 3 errors against B0's 1, on 384 contexts — is that it is noise. It is recorded rather than interpreted.

The same conservative reflex shows up in both model families

Two model families, two magnitudes, one direction.

Of the 16 contexts B0 gets wrong, B1 fixes 11 and breaks 6 that B0 had right; 5 defeat all three conditions. On an arm with 16 errors to work with, that net of five is the whole +1.04 pp.

4. Why M0−B0 must be reported as a direction, not an interval

Under intention-to-evaluate the exploratory contrast is −1.46 pp with an interval of [−2.92 · −0.21] that excludes zero. It does not survive the pre-specified valid-only sensitivity.

Three rejected replies decide it, and all three were substantively right

The scorer rejected 3 of the 1,440 responses for a repeated identifier in decisive_event_ids, where the schema requires uniqueItems. All three chose the correct reaction, TAILOR_SUPPORT under R09_SECONDARY_BARRIER; the duplicate is always the first identifier, listed twice. Two of the three are M0. Under intention-to-evaluate they count as failures, which is exactly what pushes M0 down far enough for the interval to clear zero.

So the finding rests on a schema technicality applied to answers that were correct. Terra should be reported as replicating the direction of the open-loop penalty — the sixth negative measurement across three model families — and not as a second interval excluding zero. Qwen3's reserved result is unaffected: its valid-only sensitivity moves every estimate by less than half a point.

The defect itself is the familiar one in a new guise: the runner recorded all 1,440 calls as COMPLETED_VALID because its terminal state does not incorporate the scorer's schema check. The validity figures here are the scorer's.

What this arm establishes

Establishes. That the benchmark saturates on a frontier model, with the classification confirmed on reserved rather than development data. That the saturation is localised: four template families are solved outright and TEMPORAL_GAP carries the entire error budget. That the open-loop memory penalty appears again in the same direction, for the sixth time across three model families. And that the instrument's calibration range is now legible — 96.67% here, 74.17% for KIMI2 where the benchmark was calibrated, 29.38% for Qwen3.

Does not establish. Anything about the cognitive-memory effect. The primary contrast on this arm is uninformative by construction, and was declared so before the split was opened. No subgroup contrast is claimed. The confirmatory claim of the amendment rests on Qwen3 alone and is untouched by this document.
