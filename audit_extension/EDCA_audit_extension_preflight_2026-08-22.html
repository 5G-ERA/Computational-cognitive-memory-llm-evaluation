Experiment 1 Audit Preflight

Four of the extension's checks were deciding their own estimands. Twenty-six decisions later the artefacts are gone, the gate reads GO, and both reserved splits have been audited: 2,880 calls, 4,800 cases. A self-audit then overturned one of the run's own headlines and quantified the other. What survives is narrow, exploratory and model-specific — and it is something decision accuracy could not have shown.

Findings that change the protocol

Each is measured, not argued. Identifiers match PROPOSED_AMENDMENTS.md in the package.

A2 Resolved

Once A1 is fixed, Level 1 sits at a ceiling and cannot discriminate

97.5%–100% in every condition, both models. Structural: the public response schema has four fields and the frozen runner already rejects schema-invalid responses, so nearly any response that reaches the audit is Level 1 conformant. This is the same shape as the Terra ceiling in the decision experiment — the instrument is sound, the contrast has no room to move.

Recommended: keep ΔIO but declare it an instrument-integrity endpoint, not a discriminating one, and state the ceiling. A ceiling here is itself the evidence that the retained artefacts support reconstruction in every condition.

Resolved

Applied: the Level 1 differences are declared INSTRUMENT_INTEGRITY and non-confirmatory in contracts/terminology_audit.json. Every generated report now states the ceiling as a mandatory caveat.

A3 Resolved

§8.3 makes the UNRESOLVED family a critical violation by construction

Exactly 10 of 40 memories carry constitutive_event_ids: []. They are exactly the 10 whose verification state is UNRESOLVED — one per assumption family, by design. An unresolved verification has no constitutive evidence because it was never resolved; that is its resolved state. §8.3 calls that a critical violation.

A4 Resolved

The principal Level 2 estimand is degenerate on this parent release

§8.1 says fields absent from the release must be marked NOT_RECORDED_IN_SOURCE_RELEASE and never inferred. §7.2 and §8.3 make several of those same fields mandatory components. On v0.5.1-qa1c the two instructions conflict, so the implementation computes both readings side by side and chooses neither for you.

ReadingM0 L2 conformityB1 L2 conformityB1 criticalΔTrace
LITERAL0.00%0.00%100.00%+0.00 [+0.00, +0.00]
SCOPED100.00%74–75%25.00%−25 (the A3 artefact)
 Under LITERAL the estimand cannot move: zero point, zero-width interval, both models. Under SCOPED it is A3. Neither reading measures a system property.

The cause runs deeper than the wording. The release has no trace-writing path. The M0 and B1 objects are delivered to the model inside the payload; the model returns reaction, rule_id, decisive_event_ids and reason — nothing else. So Level 2 as specified audits the dataset, which the §14 checks confirm is invariant across all five contexts, all three renderings and both models, for every memory.

That is also why the protocol reads as if it were written for Experiment 2: there the system writes a lifecycle trace κt and equation (36) can compare it with a frozen reference. Experiment 1 has no κt.

Two clean ways out. (1) Report Level 2 as a release-integrity audit — the retained artefacts are complete, coherent and non-rewritten — stated once for the release, and drop equation (7). Implementable today; it is what the current numbers actually support. (2) Extend the public response schema so the model returns the resolution state, the evidence it relied on, the current relation it judged to hold and its grounds for current use — then equation (7) measures a system property. That needs new calls and a new prospectively frozen protocol, which §21 already anticipates. Recommended: (1) now, (2) declared as the follow-on.

Resolved

Applied: RELEASE-INTEGRITY NOW, EXTEND SCHEMA AS FOLLOW-ON. Equation (7) is withdrawn from the confirmatory set and the Level 2 result now lives in audit_release_integrity.csv, one row per memory. The schema extension is declared as the §21 follow-on.

A7Resolved

Two gate conditions this package cannot meet alone

Independent-validator agreement needs a second implementation written from contracts/ and schemas/ by someone who does not read scripts/. Human-review reliability needs a frozen rubric and a coded development sample. Until both exist, every conformity figure is a deterministic-only conformity and therefore an upper bound: adding the human layer can only lower it. Both are recorded as unmet, never quietly scored.

Resolved

Two of the three remain unmet, and decision C2 added a fourth: reserved audit execution now needs its own signed authorisation record. Adrian is writing the independent validator from the contracts alone; his pack excludes scripts/, and his fixtures are blind.

A8Resolved

This "audit conformity" is not equation (36)

V4.7 defines audit conformity as the realised lifecycle trace matching an independently specified frozen reference certificate. This extension is oracle-independent by design and has no certificate to match, so it measures the rung below — functional auditability plus internal coherence of the system's own stated pathway. That is the right thing to measure here, and it is what §1 of the protocol says, but the two constructs must not share a name in the paper. Suggested: trace reconstructibility and trace coherence, with audit conformity reserved for equation (36).

Resolved

Applied: contracts/terminology_audit.json. Level 1 reports input–output trace reconstructibility, Level 2 reports cognitive-trace coherence, the §13 reconstructibility metric becomes functional auditability, and audit conformity is reserved for equation (36).

The effect is graded, and the gradient is the interesting part. Giving Qwen3 the richer memory object destabilises which evidence it points to most (−20.0 pp), which rule it invokes next (−12.5), and what it decides least (−8.1, not distinguishable from zero). The decision survives the rewording better than its stated grounds do. And it is spread across the sample — worse in 21 of 32 memories, not concentrated in a few.

Terra shows none of it: flat on all three measures, every interval spanning zero. So this is not a property of cognitive memory as such. It is a property of how one particular model consumes it — which is exactly the kind of thing a functional audit exists to localise, and exactly the kind of thing decision accuracy alone would never have shown.

Both critical violations come from one memory

Of 4,800 audited cases, two carried a critical violation. Both are Qwen3, both on MEM51-0039, and both are the same defect: the returned rule cannot produce the returned reaction. One also failed the public response schema. Terra produced none in 2,400 cases. A localisable defect on a single memory is exactly what a functional audit is for.

Boundary. The reserved splits for Terra and Qwen3 were audited under AUTHORISATION_reserved_audit_001, signed delegated. The Kimi arm has its own unsigned record and has not been run. The audit reads no accuracy figure at any point and no join between accuracy and audit results has been performed. Sections 16 and the §17 reimplementation requirement are retired by decision, each with its cost declared: the audit takes no human input, and every figure rests on a single implementation.
